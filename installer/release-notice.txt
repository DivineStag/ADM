ADM - Aircraft Dispatch Manager

ADM is freeware. You may use official releases free of charge. Source reuse and
redistribution require permission under the accompanying ADM freeware licence.
Third-party components and sounds retain their own licence terms.

This installer includes the application and required runtime. Pilots do not need
the MSFS SDK or a separate .NET installation. MSFS 2024 and GSX are installed
separately. Disable competing GSX service automation in Any2GSX while retaining
the aircraft's required integration.

ADM installs for your Windows account and keeps personal profiles/history when
uninstalled. Boarding requires your approval. The final door question is a
conversational sign-off; physical doors, equipment and pushback remain with you
and GSX. No Microsoft, Asobo or FSDT endorsement is implied.
