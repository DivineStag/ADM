ADM - Aircraft Dispatch Manager

Local qualification beta

This installer includes the application and its runtime. You do not need to install the MSFS SDK, .NET or development tools. MSFS 2024 and GSX must be installed separately.

ADM installs for your Windows account. Your profiles and history are stored separately and kept when uninstalling.

This local beta is unsigned. It has not been qualified for public distribution. Runtime redistribution permissions, supplied media rights and the live aircraft test matrix remain under review.

Pushback always belongs to the pilot.
